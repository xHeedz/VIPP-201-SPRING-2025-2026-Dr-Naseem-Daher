- art. 54 RGC: distance that allows stopping without collision if the vehicle ahead brakes suddenly; not keeping it: 200 EUR and 4 points.
- DGT rule of thumb: 2 seconds behind the vehicle ahead, 3 in adverse conditions; in tunnels at least 100 m or 4 s.
- limits used by UAH trips: motorway 120 km/h (UAH OSM file column 1), secondary roads 90 km/h.
