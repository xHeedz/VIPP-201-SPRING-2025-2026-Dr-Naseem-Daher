# greece

- KOK limits in 2018 (pNEUMA): 50 km/h in built up areas, 90 rural, 110 expressway, 130 motorway.
- from 1 Jan 2026 the default urban limit is 30 km/h, with 50 km/h kept on multi lane urban roads; pNEUMA predates this.
