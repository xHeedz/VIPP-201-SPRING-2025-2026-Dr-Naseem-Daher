# germany

- half the speedometer reading in metres, for example 50 m at 100 km/h, which is a time gap of 1.8 s.
- fines (up to 130 km/h): below 5/10 of it 75 EUR and 1 point, down to below 1/10 320 EUR; above 130 km/h higher, with driving bans.
- 130 km/h on motorways is the advisory speed (Richtgeschwindigkeit), not a general limit.
