# united states (california)

- CVC 21703: a driver shall not follow more closely than is reasonable and prudent, having due regard for speed, traffic and road.
- DMV handbook: count three seconds after the vehicle ahead passes a fixed point; increase to 4 to 6 seconds in adverse conditions.
- speed limits here are typical posted values (65 mph freeway = 105 km/h, 35 mph arterial = 56 km/h); the NGSIM sites' posted limits are not in the data and are not verified.
